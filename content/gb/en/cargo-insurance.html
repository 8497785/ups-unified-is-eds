Frequently Asked Questions

Who should purchase cargo insurance?

Cargo insurance can be an important safeguard for businesses shipping goods with a value that exceeds standard carrier liability. It helps reduce financial exposure from loss, damage or theft during transit.

What does cargo insurance coverage include?

Cargo insurance typically covers loss or damage to goods during transit by air, sea, rail or road. While carriers aim to deliver shipments safely, unforeseen events can occur. Shipping insurance can help protect your business in these situations, subject to policy terms.

What types of shipments can InsureShield cover?

We can provide cargo insurance coverage for a wide range of business shipments. Some policies may require special adjustments for certain goods. Each customer is assessed individually based on the business’ needs.

If you have questions about specific shipments and businesses, get in touch and we can help clarify your options.

Do you work with small or large businesses?

Our cargo insurance services are designed to support both small and large businesses. Whether you ship high-value goods regularly or manage high‑volume freight, we can help you explore cargo insurance options that align with your business.

How is carrier liability different from cargo insurance?

Carrier liability is typically included as part of a shipping service and offers limited compensation if goods are lost or damaged in transit. Shipping insurance can provide broader protection for the insured value of goods and may cover a wider range of transportation‑related risks. This could help businesses better manage potential financial exposure.

What is your process for getting a policy?

Start by getting in touch to discuss your shipping activity, including shipment values, destinations and transport modes. We can help guide you through the application process and outline shipping insurance options that may be suitable for your business needs.
