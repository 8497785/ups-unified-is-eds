Insuring Better Shipping Experiences™

Shipping protection that does the heavy lifting for you.

What it is

InsureShield® shipping insurance, offered through UPS Capital Insurance Agency, Inc. and its affiliates, is multi-carrier, multi-modal protection for in-transit goods. Package and freight claims can be a lengthy process, often extending for multiple weeks and several rounds of communication with a carrier. With InsureShield protection, submitting claims is easy with payments in days not weeks. This allows you to focus on what matters most - growing your business.

Why it Matters

Today’s supply chains are longer and more complex, inviting more risk for loss and damage. Our team understands the rigors of transportation and can craft a policy that covers all, or some, of your goods. Shipping insurance can offer you peace of mind and let you proactively take care of your customer when there is a shipping mishap.

How we can Help

With our logistics expertise, we’ll discuss how you make, move and sell your goods as well as your risk tolerance. From there, we can craft a policy that meets your needs, including air and ocean freight, FTL/LTL and parcel. With leading insuretech and global supply chain expertise, you’ll get valuable insights to better understand risks and protect your supply chain.
