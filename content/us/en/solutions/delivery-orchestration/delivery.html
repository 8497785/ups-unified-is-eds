Intelligent Orchestration

Create a customized dispatch strategy with dynamic rule-based automation for batching, carrier selection, and scheduling. Optimize based on factors live delivery network coverage, cost efficiency, speed, and other key attributes.

Flexible Order Batching

Batching enhances control over dispatch operations, delivery service provider (DSP) selection, and overall order management. Orders within a batch can be automatically processed and prioritized based on various criteria for optimized efficiency.

Pickup Locations

Customize pickup locations for DSPs to align with your business needs and operational flow. Choose from storefronts, warehouses, or distribution centers (DCs) to optimize fulfillment efficiency.

Custom Coverage

Retailers can define precise delivery zones by setting a coverage radius, customizing service areas within polygons or zip codes, and restricting specific regions from eligibility as needed.

Delivery Assurance

Verify delivery availability before finalizing an order. Retail teams can assess and confirm eligible delivery options for both in-store and online purchases.

Analytics & Insights

Bulit-in reports provide instant business insights with no setup required. Advanced models, continuously refined using historical delivery data, offer accurate ETA and ETD predictions across the platform. Reports can also be easily exported to BI tools or Excel for deeper analysis.
