FAQ

Why is reverse logistics tracking important?

Reverse logistics tracking helps businesses and customers see return progress, while also reducing delays and customer support requests.

How does a return management system help customers?

A returns management system helps with automating return labels and speeding up the returns process. This makes the process easy for both businesses and customers.

How can businesses reduce the costs on returns?

Using our custom returns management system helps your customers find drop-off and access points easily for their returns. This gives them more control and reduces costs for your business. This also will allow you and your business to better manage disposition to categorize any returned products easily and efficiently.
