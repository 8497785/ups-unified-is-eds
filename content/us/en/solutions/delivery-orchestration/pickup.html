FAQ

What is pickup management and how does it help retailers?

Pickup management allows businesses to offer curbside, in-store pickup, and BOPA (buy online, pick up anywhere) options. This can help make order fulfilment more efficient and convenient for customers.

How can businesses enhance curbside and in-store pick up?

Our pickup software and solutions make your fulfilment operations easier by letting customers pick up their orders their way. It connects easily with global carriers and delivery solutions and reduces wait times with real-time orchestration. These benefits make curbside pickup software easy to use for both retailers and customers.

What are the benefits of using delivery logistics software for pickup management?

Using delivery logistics software for pickup gives customers more choices. It also reduces wait times and improves collaboration between customers and stores. This enhances last-mile logistics and improves customer satisfaction. In addition to benefitting customers, using delivery logistics software for pickup also gives businesses an easy way to manage their pickups online.
