Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about how CommerceShield can protect your business

What is the CommerceShield tool and how does it work?

CommerceShield is a multi-signal fraud prevention and chargeback protection integration that analyzes every order using machine learning and data including UPS address scoring, identity verification, device fingerprinting, and payment data.

It provides a 100-1000 risk score for each transaction, enabling you to identify potential fraud before fulfillment.

How is this different from traditional fraud prevention tools?

Unlike traditional tools that only catch stolen credit cards, the CommerceShieldTM tool is specifically designed for first-party fraud; false non-receipt claims and chargeback abuse.

Instead of just sending alerts, it automatically acts by orchestrating delivery types based on your business rules, preventing losses before they occur.

Can the CommerceShield tool integrate with my existing e-commerce platform?

Yes, the CommerceShieldTM solution integrates with major e-commerce platforms like Shopify, WooCommerce, and more; and works across all carriers and sales channels. Our system connects fraud detection, fulfillment, and delivery risk in a single decision layer.

What kind of support do you provide during implementation?

We provide dedicated implementation support to help you configure risk thresholds, set up business rules, and integrate with your existing systems. Our team works with you to ensure optimal settings for your specific business needs and order patterns.

How does the CommerceShield solution differ from shipping insurance?

The CommerceShield™ solution can help multiple business leaders achieve their goals:

  • Risk and Fraud Leaders gain greater visibility into transaction-level risk and can proactively identify potentially fraudulent orders before they result in losses.
  • Operations and Logistics Leaders can make more informed fulfillment decisions, helping reduce shipments associated with high-risk transactions and false non-receipt claims.
  • Finance Leaders can help minimize chargeback-related costs, revenue leakage, and operational expenses associated with dispute management.
  • E-commerce and Digital Commerce Leaders can balance fraud prevention with customer experience by applying risk-based fulfillment strategies instead of relying on broad order declines.
  • Customer Experience Leaders can help reduce friction for legitimate customers while supporting efforts to prevent abusive claims and fraudulent activity.

How does the CommerceShield solution differ from shipping insurance?

CommerceShieldTM helps prevent fraud before an order is fulfilled by analyzing transactions and identifying high-risk behavior. Shipping Insurance protects the physical shipment if it's lost, damaged, or stolen after it ships. Shipping Insurance does not reimburse the financial losses from the result of chargebacks.

How much does the CommerceShield service cost?

The CommerceShield™ service starts at just $0.30 per order. A business processing 1,000 scored orders per month would typically pay about $300 per month.

Pricing can be customized based on order volume, integrations, and risk strategy requirements.Speak with a risk expert to receive a personalized assessment and pricing recommendation for your business.
