FAQ

What is delivery orchestration?

Delivery orchestration is the process of managing several different carriers, fulfillment locations, and shipping options within an omnichannel e-commerce platform. This process helps you achieve seamlessness and efficiency across your business.

How does delivery orchestration improve last-mile logistics?

It provides efficient routing, real-time tracking, and smooth carrier integrations. This improves on-time delivery and lowers costs.

How does an order and delivery management system make the post-purchase experience better?

An order and delivery management system helps retailers provide accurate tracking, estimated delivery windows, and notifications. This improves the post-purchase experience for customers and reduces the amount of customer service inquiries on orders.
