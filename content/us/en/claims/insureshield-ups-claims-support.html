Claims Made Easy

Set yourself up for success by registering for our InsureShield® Online Portal for complete claims & billing visibility and see how easy it is to get your package covered.

How Do I Register For the Online Portal?

Set up your account by visiting our registration page
Enter your policy ID, email Address and zip code
Select whether you’re the Account Holder, Billing Contact or Claims Contact
Click Register

Once registered, you will have access to file claims, view claim status, and manage your InsureShield account.

How Do I Insure My Shipment?

To ensure your shipments are insured, you must declare a value for each package by following the steps below or watch our tutorial.

Enter the delivery address into your shipping software (UPS WorldShip, CampusShip, etc.)
Select the delivery service (Ground, Next Day Air, etc.)
Enter the package weight
Enter the value of the package contents
Print the shipping label

Be sure to accurately declare the value of your package contents so you are properly insured in case of loss or damage.
