Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about how Risk Intelligence can protect your business

What is Risk Intelligence and how does it work?

Risk Intelligence is a multi-signal fraud prevention and chargeback protection integration that analyzes every order using machine learning and data including UPS address scoring, identity verification, device fingerprinting, and payment data.

It provides a 100-1000 risk score for each transaction, enabling you to identify potential fraud before fulfillment.

How is this different from traditional fraud prevention tools?

Unlike traditional tools that only catch stolen credit cards, Risk Intelligence is specifically designed for first-party fraud; false non-receipt claims and chargeback abuse.

Instead of just sending alerts, it automatically acts by orchestrating delivery types based on your business rules, preventing losses before they occur.

What is the chargeback reimbursement coverage?

For orders scoring 700+ that still result in chargebacks, we cover up to the full order value. Our fast resolution process means you get paid in days instead of weeks, protecting your revenue and cash flow.

Can Risk Intelligence integrate with my existing e-commerce platform?

Yes, Risk Intelligence integrates with major e-commerce platforms like Shopify, WooCommerce, and more; and works across all carriers and sales channels. Our system connects fraud detection, fulfillment, and delivery risk in a single decision layer.

What kind of support do you provide during implementation?

We provide dedicated implementation support to help you configure risk thresholds, set up business rules, and integrate with your existing systems. Our team works with you to ensure optimal settings for your specific business needs and order patterns.

How does Risk Intelligence differ from Shipping Insurance?

Risk Intelligence helps prevent fraud before an order is fulfilled by analyzing transactions and identifying high-risk behavior. Shipping Insurance protects the physical shipment if it's lost, damaged, or stolen after it ships. Shipping Insurance does not cover the financial losses from the result of chargebacks.

How does Risk Intelligent fit into my overall "Risk Strategy"?

Risk Intelligence adds a proactive layer to your risk management strategy. Think of protection in layers:

1. Prevention - Risk Intelligence identifies high-risk orders before fulfillment
2. Financial Protection - Chargeback Guarantee covers eligible disputes
3. Shipment Protection - Shipping Insurance covers loss or damage in transit.

Together, these layers reduce revenue loss, operational overhead, and chargeback exposure.
