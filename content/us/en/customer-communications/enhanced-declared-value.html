Enhanced Declared Value

Protection for UPS® Shipments

Enhanced Declared Value helps protect the value of eligible UPS shipments if they are lost or damaged during transit. Enhanced Declared Value provides an added layer of protection that can help businesses recover the value of eligible shipments and enhance the customer experience when the unexpected happens. For 3PLs, it offers a practical way to help protect customer shipments, support operational efficiency, and strengthen customer relationships.

Helps Protect High-Value Shipments:

Coverage for eligible UPS shipments valued up to $50,000, subject to program terms, conditions, exclusions, and availability.

Easy to Add:

Fits directly into the UPS shipping workflow

Why Protection Matters

Shipping protection isn't just about protecting packages. It's about protecting customer relationships, supporting business growth, and delivering a better experience for the brands that trust you with their fulfillment operations.

Strengthen Customer Relationships

When an order doesn't arrive as expected, your client's customer may look to them for answers, and your client may look to you. Protection solutions can help support a better customer experience when issues occur.

Reduce Costly Shipping Expenses & Reshipments

Lost or damaged shipments can result in unexpected expenses, including product replacement, reshipping, and customer concessions that impact profitability.

Protect Brand Reputation

Your customers trust you to deliver a reliable fulfillment experience. Shipping issues, even when outside your direct control, can affect confidence in your services and your ability to win new business.
