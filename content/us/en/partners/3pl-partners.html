When a shipment goes wrong, your customer calls you

Your customer may not know which carrier caused the problem or what the carrier will pay. They know that they trusted you to manage the shipment.
InsureShield Shipping Insurance gives you a protection option you can offer with clear next steps instead of sending the customer to a carrier, an outside insurer, or another disconnected process.

Give customers another reason to choose you

Offer shipping protection alongside the transportation, fulfillment, and technology your customers already depend on.

Keep recovery from becoming a relationship problem

A lost or damaged shipment creates replacement work, customer calls, and reshipping decisions. Take the hassle out of their hands with a streamlined claims experience.

Keep the experience connected

Customers can access shipping protection through the platform and workflow they already use, rather than purchasing it through an unrelated provider.

Customer-facing value

Help customers recover more than carrier liability may provide

Carrier liability and shipping insurance are not the same thing. Carrier liability generally depends on the carrier being responsible for the loss or damage, and the amount recovered may not reflect what the goods were worth. InsureShield shipping insurance provides a separate shipping insurance path for eligible shipments, subject to the applicable policy terms, conditions, and exclusions.

With shipping protection available

  • Up to the full invoice value of the shipment is covered
  • Insurance is natively integrated into your platform
  • Claims can be filed 24/7 through an online portal
  • Claims are paid in days, not weeks*
