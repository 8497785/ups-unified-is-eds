Common questions about shipping insurance

Learn how shipping insurance works, what may be covered, and which option could fit your business.

InsureShield® is a shipping insurance program underwritten through UPS Capital Insurance Agency, Inc. It covers the full insured value of your shipments against loss, damage, and porch pirated theft (across UPS®, FedEx®, DHL®, USPS®, and other LTL and multimodal carriers) with most claims resolved within 5 business days.

InsureShield® covers loss, damage, and porch piracy up to the full sales value of your shipment, including original shipping costs. Expedited reshipping reimbursement is also available for qualifying shipments. Coverage is available for domestic and international shipments depending on jurisdiction.

Carrier liability, such as UPS® or FedEx® declared value, is not insurance. It is governed by each carrier’s published terms, limits, and exclusions. InsureShield® is shipping insurance that provides broader protection for eligible shipments and can be customized to meet your business’s needs, including specialty coverage options. Most claims are paid in days, not weeks. Coverage and claim payments are subject to the applicable policy terms.

Yes. Customers can opt into InsureShield® shipping protection when they complete their purchase. The cost of coverage is presented separately at checkout and paid by the customer, while your business gains another way to address eligible lost or damaged shipments. It integrates easily with Shopify carts and other ecommerce platforms via API.

Claims can be filed online through the InsureShield® claims portal. You will need the shipment tracking number, the insured value, and documentation of the loss or damage (photos, carrier notification, etc.). Most claims are processed in a matter of days. InsureShield® customers have access to direct communication with a claims specialist.

Yes, InsureShield® shipping insurance supports eligible domestic and international shipments. InsureShield® shipping insurance may be available to eligible businesses in Ontario, with availability in additional Canadian provinces planned for 2027. Options are also available in select international markets, including the United Kingdom, France, Germany, and Hong Kong. Availability varies by jurisdiction, shipping route, shipment type, and applicable policy terms.
