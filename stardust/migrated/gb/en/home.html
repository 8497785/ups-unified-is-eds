How InsureShield Commercial Shipping Insurance Works

Quote

Based on your needs, we’ll provide a policy structure and quote for your shipping insurance.

Protect

We offer multi-carrier coverage, so you can ship via UPS or other carriers with protection tailored to your business. Multi-modal coverage is available for packaging travelling by road, air or ocean.

Integrate

Use our API or webhooks to automate insurance through your e-commerce platforms, or handle everything through our online portal.

Claim

Our dedicated claims team works efficiently to help you recover eligible losses quickly, when needed.
