Small Package Shipments for Gallagher Affinity

Last Updated December 20, 2023

In particular, you should ensure that your shipments are eligible for coverage and that you understand the terms of protection offered under the Program. Your acceptance of these T&Cs is a condition precedent for your goods to be protected under the Program. In addition, you should fully review and understand each of the following before you request to participate in the Program with respect to any shipment of goods: the UPS Capital Group Technology Agreement (the current version of which is available at https://www.insureshield.com/us/en/legal/technology-agreement.html), and the UPS Capital Privacy Notice (the current version of which is available at https://www.insureshield.com/us/en/legal/privacy-notice.html) (together with these T&Cs, collectively, the Acceptance Documents). Each time that you participate in the Program, you will be deemed to have accepted and agreed to the versions of the Acceptance Documents in effect at the time the protection was requested or deemed to be requested (or, if the request or deemed request relates to a general election to protect future shipments of goods, the versions of such Acceptance Documents in effect at the time of booking of the applicable shipment of goods).

2.      OFFER TO INCLUDE GOODS UNDER POLICY: Channel Partner has provided you and other of its members (Members) the opportunity to participate in the Program by protecting your eligible shipments of goods under one or more cargo insurance policies (Policies) issued through UPSCIA to Channel Partner. Your election to include your goods under the Program is entirely optional. You are not permitted to offer any other party the opportunity to participate in the Program and any such offer will be null and void. Channel Partner further reserves the right to decline or exclude certain shipments from protection under the Policies.

3.      POLICY MEMBER GROUPS:  Protection under the Policies may extend to, and vary between, packages of four Member categories: (i) Group 1, which includes, but is not limited to, such market sectors as industrial manufacturing, automotive, construction and engineering; (ii) Group 2, which includes, but is not limited to, such market sectors as consumer services, chambers, buying groups, corporate partners, communications, entrepreneurial, fraternal, non-profit and travel/hospitality; (iii) Group 3, which includes, but is not limited to, such market sectors as professional services members, financial/insurance, real estate, legal, technology and healthcare; and (iv) Group 4, which includes, but is not limited to, such market sectors as food, beverage, pharmaceuticals, and perishables. As set forth herein, not all Members are eligible for the same protection under the Program.  If you have any questions as to which Group or Policy applies to you, please contact UPSCIA for more information.

4.      CERTAIN END USERS AND SHIPMENTS NOT ELIGIBLE: Only prepaid outbound packages shipped with United Parcel Service, Inc. (“UPS”) and inbound freight collect and inbound third party billed shipments shipped with UPS and originated in the U.S. and billed under a UPS shipper account number using a UPS Package Level Detail (“PLD”) compliant shipping system are eligible for coverage under the Policies. End Users located or based outside of the United States and retail shipping outlets (whether they are acting for themselves or for any of their respective customers) are not eligible to participate in the Program. With respect to any End User that elects to participate in the Program for a business purpose, shipments with contents unrelated to such business purpose will not be protected under any Policy regardless of commodity type. End Users, who are individual persons, are not eligible to participate in the Program except to the extent acting on behalf of a corporate or similar business entity for a business purpose.

5.      REQUESTING PROTECTION UNDER A POLICY: To request protection under a Policy for any shipment, the shipper must insert the insurable value (Insured Value) in the Declared Value field in the UPS shipping system or an approved reference field, in accordance with Section 13(c) below. If the shipper fails to insert the Insured Value in the Declared Value or approved referenced field in the shipping system when manifesting the package for transport, then such package will not be included for protection under any of the Policies.

(a)    For any package consigned to a residence, the shipper is required to specify “Signature Required for Delivery” when manifesting the package for transport if the Insured Value is $5,000 or more. Failure to specify “Signature Required for Delivery” when required shall result in the package not being included under any of the Policies. If the recipient to whom the package is consigned allows UPS to effect delivery without a signature where such specification is required hereunder, protection under the Policies will be null and void.

(b)    For any packages shipped via a UPS internet shipping account with an Insured Value exceeding $1,000, the shipper must obtain a driver signature on a “Proof of Pickup” at the time of package pickup. Failure by the shipper to obtain a “Proof of Pickup” at the time of package pickup may result in declination of coverage under the Policies.

(c)    For any package with an Insured Value of $100 or less, the shipper must insert an Insured Value of $101 in the Declared Value field of the UPS shipping system.  Failure to do so will result in such package not being included for protection under any of the Policies.

(d)    For a shipment comprised of multiple packages being shipped under a single source document consigned to the same receiver, a single aggregate Insured Value for the entire multi-piece shipment may be declared in the Declared Value field corresponding to the first package of the shipment.  If so declared, no amount should be declared in the Declared Value field for any package except the first package.

6.      NO RIGHTS UNDER THE POLICIES: This offer is not intended to and does not provide insurance directly to you. You are neither an insured nor an additional insured nor do you have any other rights under any Policy. In the event of a loss covered under an applicable Policy, any resulting claim payment may be directed to you by Channel Partner as a loss payee and only to the extent of your interest in the protected shipment.

All descriptions or illustrations of coverage provided herein are for general informational purposes only, are not statements of contract and do not amend, alter or modify any Policy. For a complete understanding of the protection available, please review the Policies for their exact terms, conditions, definitions, and exclusions.

We may communicate or otherwise interact with you from time to time to facilitate Channel Partner’s administration of the Program, including by delivering invoices to you for shipment protection-related fees; receiving payments from you; receiving from you, and administering, claims under the Program; and delivering claim payments to you at the request of Channel Partner. No such communications or interactions shall be deemed to create a broker or customer relationship between you and UPSCIA or our affiliates.

10.    COMPLIANCE WITH TRANSPORTATION CARRIER TERMS AND CONDITIONS: Your compliance with the transportation carrier’s shipping terms and conditions in effect at time of shipment shall be a condition precedent for protection under the Program. In accepting the transportation carrier’s shipping terms and conditions and these T&Cs, you do so on behalf of yourself and all parties with any interest in the goods.

11.    ELIGIBLE CONVEYANCES AND ROUTES: Protection under the Policies applies exclusively to U.S. origin shipments being shipped to Domestic U.S. and/or International destinations, provided that protection will be null and void whenever it (or any related payment or other transaction) would be in violation of any U.S. economic or trade sanctions.. Domestic U.S. shall include the continental U.S., Alaska, Hawaii and Puerto Rico. International destinations shall include countries outside of the previously defined Domestic U.S.

12.    LIMITS, DEDUCTIBLES AND FEES: The Policies may provide for different limits, deductibles, and fees depending upon the commodity types being shipped. Note that motorized vehicles are only eligible for “Free of Particular Average” or “FPA” coverage, meaning that only the risk of total loss is covered in most instances for such commodity types and not the risk of partial loss or damage. If you are asked to indicate the type of commodity being shipped, when requesting protection for shipments containing multiple commodity types, choose the commodity representing the majority of items in the shipment, but each specific commodity type will remain subject to all the terms and restrictions applicable to that specific commodity type. Notwithstanding the foregoing, protection under the Program is limited in all events to $50,000 per package, subject to the following sub-limits:

(a)    Domestic U.S. packages – $50,000 per package;

(b)    International packages – $50,000 per package;

(c)    International packages containing any jewelry – $500 per package;

(d)    Packages shipped by a shipper via a UPS internet shipping account – $50,000 per package for regular pickup accounts or $5,000 for any other pickups;

(e)    UPS Print Return Label Services – $999 per package;

(f)     UPS Pickup Attempt Return Services and UPS Call Tag Service – $50,000 per package;

(g)    Coverage for Eligible Consequential Losses (Group 3 and Group 4 Members only) – up to $500 per package;

(h)    Aggregate limit – $1,000,000 per conveyance per each of the Policies (applicable to any single conveyance with multiple shipments of one or multiple Members).

13.    SCOPE OF PROTECTION: The Program provides shipment protection for physical loss and/or damage to package contents in the course of transit, subject to Program conditions, limitations and exclusions. The Program provides no protection for delay in transit or for consequential or indirect losses, except the limited consequential coverage provided to Group 3 and Group 4 Members as summarized below. The scope of shipment protection under the Program is summarized as follows:

(a)    Duration: Shipment protection under the Program commences upon UPS acquiring a physical scan of the package and continues in the course of transit until such time as delivery is made at the consigned destination or until 30 days after transit is terminated at any place, whichever is sooner. Delivery is made when a package is signed for at the consigned location or when the package is left at the consigned location without a signature, as determined by the carrier’s tracking information. Shipments signed for by any apparent representative (e.g., concierge, doorman, front desk clerk, residence occupant, security guard) at the delivery address shall be deemed delivered. Notwithstanding the foregoing, protection may be cancelled in the course of transit in the event that a Member, shipper, or consignee do not cooperate or are unresponsive to facilitate or accept delivery; in such event, it is the Member’s burden as loss payee to prove any covered loss occurred.

(b)    Limited Consequential Coverage for Group 3 and Group 4 Members Only: In the event of loss or damage to a package that is covered under one of the Policies for Group 3 and Group 4 Members, consequential losses associated therewith shall be additionally covered subject to a maximum amount of $500.00 in covered consequential losses; however, eligible consequential losses are limited to costs of document reconstruction or re-issuance; reshipping fees; late penalty fees; or the face value of gift certificates and game / sporting event tickets.

(c)    Valuation: The shipper must declare an Insured Value of the invoice price, or their selling price if not the same as the amount invoiced to the consigned recipient of the package. Group 3 and Group 4 Members seeking coverage for eligible consequential losses must add the coverage amount desired for consequential losses to the Insured Value declared. Any protection under the Policies will be limited in all events to the lowest of Insured Value declared, the correct valuation, or the actual covered losses incurred. Loss valuations are subject to substantiation, and no Policy is an agreed value policy.

(d)    Excluded Commodities and Shipments: The following commodities are not eligible for coverage under the Program: coins; currency; postage stamps; negotiated instruments (except checks); money orders; unset precious stones; industrial diamonds; any article that contains more than 50% by weight gold or platinum or any combination thereof in raw form, including but not limited to bullion, bars, or scraps of these metals; human remains and/or body parts; the face value of checks, phone cards, gift certificates or gift cards (except as may be expressly covered for Group 3 Members); and any other restricted items or commodities pursuant to the UPS Tariff (the current version of which is available at https://www.ups.com/assets/resources/webcontent/en_US/terms_service_us.pdf). Any packages having a value greater than $50,000 and/or any packages shipped using Air Shipping Documents and/or other non-PLD compliant offerings are further not eligible for coverage under the Policies. Size and weight instructions also apply: packages are excluded from coverage under the Policies if exceeding 150 pounds, or if exceeding 108 inches in length, or if exceeding a total of 165 inches in length and girth [(2 * width) + (2 * height)] combined.

(e)    Excluded Risks: Protection under the Program is subject to exclusions which include losses resulting from or attributable to:

  • war risks;
  • nuclear or radioactive contamination;
  • delay, whether or not caused by an insured peril;
  • temperature variation, whether or not caused by a covered peril;
  • inherent vice or illicit trade;
  • fraud, dishonesty or infidelity by you, the shipper or consignee;
  • misrepresentation / false information by you, the shipper or consignee;
  • capture, seizure, arrest or detainment of goods by public authority;
  • rust, oxidation, discoloration, scratching, marring, denting, chipping and/or electrical or mechanical derangement to used goods, whether or not caused by a covered peril;
  • cyber attacks;
  • consequential and indirect damages, such as loss of market, depreciation, or diminution in value as a result of repair (except as otherwise set forth herein);
  • insufficient or otherwise unsuitable packing or preparation of the cargo to withstand the ordinary incidents of transit;
  • a shipment of cargo being re-routed, stopped in transit or retrieved at a point other than the original consigned delivery point pursuant to any instruction or representation to the transport carrier by a fraudster or imposter;
  • a shipment of cargo being sent as a result of fraud or trick; and
  • package labels being changed or revised in the course of transportation for the purpose of fraudulent redirection.

14.    Claim Reporting Limitation & Claim Process: You must immediately report to Channel Partner or UPSCIA, as applicable, in writing any loss or damage to a protected shipment of cargo. As a condition precedent for shipment protection under the Program, notification of claims must in all events be submitted in writing within the following timeframes: (i) in the event of a claim for concealed loss or damage, within ninety (90) days from the date of delivery; or (ii) for all other claims, within nine (9) months from the date of delivery. In the event of a claim for non-delivery, the time-for-claim shall run from the date that the cargo was scheduled to have been delivered or should have reasonably been delivered, whichever is first in time. Loss verification will be required. You should take photos of any damaged goods, packaging and/or containers. Damaged goods, packaging and/or containers should be retained for inspection at the discretion of UPSCIA and/or the Insurer. By submitting any claim under the Program, you agree to fully cooperate with Channel Partner, UPSCIA and/or the Insurer in providing all documentation and information pertinent to such claim, including an examination under oath if requested by the Insurer. At the direction of the named insured on the applicable Policy, we will remit proceeds of claims payable under the Policies directly to you as a loss payee pursuant to remittance instructions you provide. In all other cases, we will remit claim proceeds to Channel Partner, and Channel Partner will be solely responsible for ensuring that such proceeds are paid to you.
